YTXTube Custom 2.5.0: flex mode for foldables

- Stand your foldable half open (like a laptop) while a video plays: the video moves above the fold and the lower half gets a seek bar, big play controls, captions, speed, quality, comments and up next
- Held half open like a book, the video goes on the left page and the rest on the right (can be turned off)
- Works from the fold position the phone reports, with the hinge sensor as a backup (Honor Magic V series included)
- Settings > General: turn flex mode on or off, and see what the phone reports right now
- "Full video" in flex mode fills the screen with the video until you fold or open the phone again
